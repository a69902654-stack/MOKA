# ☕ Coffee Shop | Premium Coffee E-commerce

> A modern and elegant e-commerce platform for discovering, exploring, and purchasing premium coffee products.

This project is a modern **coffee e-commerce website** designed to provide a premium and seamless shopping experience for coffee enthusiasts.

The platform allows users to explore different coffee products, compare options, add products to their cart, place orders, and track their purchases through a clean and minimal user interface.

---

WEBSITE : https://luxury-gaufre-0918fd.netlify.app/

---

## ✨ Features

### ☕ Coffee Product Catalog

Users can explore a curated collection of coffee products with detailed information including:

- Product name
- Product image
- Price
- Product category
- Roast type
- Origin
- Weight
- Product description
- Availability

Products are presented through clean and responsive product cards designed to make browsing simple and intuitive.

---

### 🔎 Product Discovery

The platform provides several ways to discover products:

- Product categories
- Search
- Filters
- Sorting
- Featured products
- Popular products

Users can quickly find the coffee that matches their preferences.

---

### 🛍️ Shopping Cart

Users can add products to their shopping cart and manage their order before checkout.

Cart functionality includes:

- Add products
- Remove products
- Change quantity
- View product prices
- Calculate subtotal
- View total order cost

---

### 💳 Checkout & Ordering

The checkout experience is designed to make the ordering process simple and straightforward.

Users can provide:

- Customer information
- Phone number
- Delivery address
- Postal information
- Additional order details

The checkout page also provides an order summary before the final confirmation.

---
